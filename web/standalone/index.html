Chess Rules

1. The Board: Chess is played on an 8x8 grid with alternating light and dark squares.

2. The Pieces: Each player starts with 16 pieces: 1 king, 1 queen, 2 rooks, 2 bishops, 2 knights, and 8 pawns.

3. Movement:

  • King: One square in any direction
  • Queen: Any number of squares in any direction
  • Rook: Any number of squares horizontally or vertically
  • Bishop: Any number of squares diagonally
  • Knight: L-shape (two squares in one direction, then one square perpendicular)
  • Pawn: Forward one square, captures diagonally

4. Special Moves:

  • Castling: King moves two squares toward a rook, which jumps over
  • En Passant: Special pawn capture under specific conditions
  • Promotion: Pawn reaching the last rank becomes any other piece

5. Check: When a king is under immediate attack by an opponent's piece.

6. Checkmate: When a king is in check and cannot escape, ending the game.

7. Draw Conditions: Stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition, or 50-move rule.
